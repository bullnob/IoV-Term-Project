01 / Observe the digital twin

Position, speed, arrivals, CPU capacity, delay budget and backlog describe a vehicle. Noisy predictions estimate each mode's energy, latency and availability.

02 / Coordinate on the graph

Each actor aggregates its neighbors' state. Its 80- and 60-neuron hidden layers score five offloading modes for every subtask.

03 / Learn from the outcome

Separate centralized critics estimate future reward. Replay, target networks and a Lyapunov cost term guide actor updates toward efficient service and smaller queues.

Scope: An executable interpretation of Algorithm 2 in Alam et al., IEEE IoT Journal (2025), DOI 10.1109/JIOT.2025.3538657. Simulator parameters, graph encoder, discrete-action gradient and warm-start choices are documented in the project. These results are synthetic simulations, not a reproduction of the paper's numerical figures or a proof of long-run queue stability.
